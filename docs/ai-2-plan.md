Planning baseline: `e709f0c`, 2026-11-17. This is the ordered work plan; it does not implement or retune AI. Dependencies: milestones 01, 02 and 04; coordinate the relevant contracts with milestone 08 as that work becomes defined.

## Existing tools to preserve

**F3 is the main inspection surface.** It already pauses the battle, offers AI decision explanations and exposes scoring details. Extend those controls and records. Diagnostics must describe the same candidates and results the AI used, remain readable during play, and avoid consuming combat randomness or changing the decision when enabled.

Existing simulation results already separate completion, stalls, round limits, timeouts and setup failures, with reproduction data. Validate those classifications before using their totals to judge intelligence. Keep fixed-seed reports and saved action replays complementary: replay verifies recorded execution; it does not automatically prove that rerunning the planner makes identical choices.

## Scope and stopping point

Retain utility scoring wherever it remains useful. The five pipeline stages describe responsibilities; they do not require five new services. Keep candidate/trace types small, planning short and existing mission/action owners authoritative. Add abstractions only for a demonstrated ownership or duplication problem. Behavior trees are not a prerequisite.

Declare the current observation/information policy explicitly; this milestone does not implicitly add fog of war, a perception simulator or omniscient difficulty bonuses. Coordinate needed future inputs with milestone 08 rather than implementing that milestone inside this one.

Success means believable, legal, explainable decisions and observable mission progress. It does not mean every unit always attacks, every wait is wrong, the hardest AI must win, or a scripted test must select one exact square. Distinguish a legitimate defensive hold, an impossible route, a policy weakness, a recoverable blockage and a technical stall.

Follow [Project Ironclad](quality/project-ironclad.md): focused checks during each change, broader verification for shared systems, explicit failure classification, unchanged valid invariants and honest separation of headless, rendered and human playtest evidence. Do not retune combat rules to conceal an AI-policy weakness. The step-1 audit is recorded separately above. Address its reproducible blockers and proceed to the step-2 baseline; this does not establish that milestone 05 is complete.
