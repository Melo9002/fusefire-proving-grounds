# Ironclad Test Contract Inventory

This inventory classifies the behavior protected by FuseFire's 92 standalone GDScript test executables. The labels describe stability, not importance: **Invariant** behavior should survive most design changes, **Contract** behavior records a current intentional rule, and **Experimental** behavior belongs to a provisional system. Mixed labels mean a test protects more than one kind of behavior.

The grouping is intentionally documentary. Test execution remains owned by `tools/run_tests.ps1`; no per-test metadata layer is required.

Uncertainty is concentrated in presentation, warehouse, debug, calibration and generation-quality tests. Their architectural separation from gameplay is invariant, but their precise UX, thresholds and appearance are experimental. Tests with exact numeric combat, AP, commander or mission expectations are mixed because their authority boundary is invariant while the numbers are current contracts.

## Five consequential coverage gaps

1. **Mixed-action replay playback.** Replay tests cover ordinary battles, rescue/departure, schema compatibility and tactical-tempo fingerprints, while Aim, Shield, Guard reactions, Reload, conditions and vertical traversal are mostly verified outside full playback. A focused recording should combine several of these actions, replay every payload, and compare authoritative fingerprints after each action. This protects a high-risk shared boundary without requiring a long battle.
2. **Battle termination immediately after mission resolution.** Objective tests validate individual transitions and rescue replay validates eventual completion, but there is no narrow contract describing exactly when action scheduling stops after the last required objective completes, which actors may still evacuate, and whether a completed mission can wait indefinitely. A small fixture should complete the final objective during an activation and assert the intended result, remaining queue behavior and absence of further unauthorized actions.
3. **Actor removal during reactions and phase advancement.** `ai_active_defeat_recovery_smoke`, Guard tests and AP lifecycle tests cover adjacent cases, but no focused fixture defeats or extracts the active actor during a reaction/consequence and then verifies occupancy cleanup, selection, initiative progression and replay identity together. This is a likely source of stalls and stale references.
4. **Mission-valid multilevel procedural layouts across seeds.** Generation tests separately cover elevation, traversal and mission placement, and batch tests cover broad validity. Goal 2 will combine them more aggressively. A future bounded multi-seed property test should require every deployed faction and required objective to share an authoritative route through explicit vertical links, while reporting the failing seed and disconnected regions.
5. **Whole-catalog bounds drift and modular compatibility.** All 315 GLBs are import-tested, but checked-in exact bounds cover only 14 curated assets. A future test should measure the representative module shortlist, detect source-pivot/dimension drift, and verify declared connectors and clearance independently of tactical occupancy. It should avoid freezing every decorative mesh or treating AABBs as collision.

## Test evolution policy

- **Regression:** reproduce it narrowly, add or extend a focused test where practical, fix the cause, then run the focused and surrounding suites.
- **Intentional rule change:** update implementation and affected contract expectations together, explain the new rule, and retain still-valid invariants.
- **Experimental redesign/removal:** replace or retire tests whose feature contract truly disappeared; record why rather than silently skipping them.
- **Flaky test:** preserve the failing seed/logs, identify nondeterminism or environment dependence, and repair the test or implementation. Quarantine only with an owner, reason and removal condition.
- **No longer relevant:** remove only after confirming no active behavior depends on the contract. Never loosen assertions solely to make the suite green.
