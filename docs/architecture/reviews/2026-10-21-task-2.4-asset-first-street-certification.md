## Objective checks

### Geometric assembly

- Real road openings are extracted from imported top-surface triangles and agree with logical connectors through rotations 0–3.
- The two straight-road bounds meet at one physical end plane. Position tolerance is `0.001` world units.
- Both road surfaces share the same top elevation.
- Each imported road sidewalk meets its imported paving tile at one physical edge and the same Y `0.11` surface top.
- The two paving tiles meet without a gap or overlap.
- Paving terminates at the calibrated physical bounds of `building-i`.
- No `RoadStreetscape` diagnostic asphalt, sidewalk, curb, or marking layer appears in normal presentation.
- Both lamps mount over a real road-sidewalk surface at Y `0.11`, face the carriageway, and remain clear of the shell.
- Actual rendered building and lamp bounds remain inside their approved envelopes in all four whole-composition rotations. The containment tolerance is `0.021` world units: `0.01` accounts for the layout's intentional boundary shrink and the remainder covers float conversion.
- A real cargo-container obstruction across the access band is rejected by frontage validation.

Road and paving ground surfaces have physical-opening and seam contracts rather than authored visual envelopes. Their actual transformed geometry is tested directly instead of fabricating redundant envelope metadata for certification.

### Cross-representation

- `building-i`'s model-local `-Z` working face agrees with planned frontage at module rotations 0–3.
- Rotating the complete reference composition maps the building and lamp frontage to explicit world directions `-X`, `-Z`, `+X`, and `+Z`.
- Canonical module rotations produce the expected physical seam planes for the road, paving, and building.
- The street-to-frontage cells remain walkable and stoppable, and Pathfinder finds a route in each whole-composition rotation.
- The shell remains non-walkable, LOS-blocking full cover beyond its exterior apron.
- Roads and paving remain ordinary ground; lamps remain visual-only. Presentation introduces no tactical occupancy, traversal, cover, or LOS.
- Repeated construction reproduces identical placement/tactical signatures and identical resolved asset paths, transforms, and quantized world bounds at every supported rotation.
