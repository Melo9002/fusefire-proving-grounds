# Generation 2.0, Task 2.3 — Street Transitions, Furniture, and Building Frontage

## Result

The initial reference slice is now a complete bounded streetscape rather than a road-only geometry probe. It uses seven authored placements: two imported straight-road tiles, two imported `tile-low` paving tiles, two imported streetlights, and the reviewed complete `building-i` exterior shell. Normal presentation contains no procedural asphalt, sidewalk, curb, or marking layer.

The fixture is available in the Map Editor as the engineering preset `asset_first_frontage` with seed `2303`. It deliberately remains outside the normal procedural district menu because this task certifies one physical relationship rather than introducing a general parcel solver.

## Authored composition

| Element | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| Street | Two `road_straight` modules at `(4,5)` and `(4,15)`; reviewed imported surfaces meet end-to-end. |
| Pedestrian transition | Two `paving_square` modules at `(14,5)` and `(14,15)`; imported `tile-low` surfaces meet the road sidewalk at the same edge and Y `0.11`. |
| Building | `industrial_shell_i` at `(24,10)`, rotation 1; its normalized frontage faces west toward the street. |
| Frontage access | Cells `(12,14)` through `(23,14)` remain walkable and stoppable from the road's inner sidewalk lane across the forecourt into the protected exterior apron. |
| Furniture | Two reviewed lamp variants at `(13,7)` and `(13,22)`, rotation 3; both stand in the outer sidewalk lane outside the entrance band and face inward. |

`building-i` remains a sealed `prebuilt_shell`. Its visible garage and double-door frontage establish architectural purpose, but no mesh-derived interior, traversal, collision, cover, or line of sight was introduced. The footprint beyond the apron remains explicit blocking `MapData`.

## Corrected presentation relationships

- Actual transformed road bounds meet each other at the longitudinal seam.
- Actual transformed road and paving bounds meet at the same X edge and top elevation.
- Both paving tiles meet one another without a gap or overlap.
- The paving terminates at the calibrated building bounds; it does not continue through the shell.
- The building's rendered model-local working face resolves to world `-X`, toward the street.
- Both lamp heads resolve to world `-X`, toward the carriageway.
- Lamp source origins represent their mounting feet. The streetlight visual contract now raises them by `0.11`, matching the imported sidewalk top. Previously their bases were buried by that amount.
- Lamp visible bounds remain clear of the building.

The lamp height correction applies to the existing `street_light` module wherever it is used. It changes presentation only. Asset calibration and original GLBs remain untouched.

## Tactical contract

`MapData` remains authoritative. Roads and paving are open ground. Lamps are visual-only. The exterior access route is checked against final `MapData` and the pathfinder. The shell cells remain non-walkable, LOS-blocking full cover. Imported meshes add no gameplay occupancy.

The paving module now publishes explicit spawn and objective candidates on known open cells. This lets bounded authored fixtures satisfy the same mission-suitability validation as the other catalog presets without deriving candidates from visible geometry.

## Content gaps and Task 2.4 handoff

- This certifies one straight-street frontage. It is not a general parcel relationship or certification system.
- The imported road's own curb terminates cleanly against flat paving, but the kit does not supply a specialized driveway ramp or crosswalk for this slice.
- Curve-to-square sidewalk and curb transitions remain uncertified.
- The building is exterior-only. Its doors are readable frontage features, not tactical entrances to an authored interior.
- Lamps remain visual-only and create no cover or collision.
- No additional furniture was added merely to fill the forecourt. Later work may add only functionally justified elements under explicit clearance contracts.
