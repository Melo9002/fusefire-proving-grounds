# Milestone 06.09: Authoritative Environmental Interactions (dynamic door), full report

Status: merged to main by the owner as PR #110 (merge commit 16354d3). Includes the final acceptance addendum. The outstanding verification results are filled in at section 9. Section 11 records the follow-up integration of the doors into the Tactical Test Range.

## 1. What shipped

One working, authoritative door (`door_a`) in the Interaction Range. The player opens and closes it with an explicit HUD button ("Open door" / "Close door", with reasons in the tooltip when unavailable). Ordinary unlocked doors cost 0 AP, any faction may operate one, and the transition is a single validated transaction that updates door state, movement geometry, standing points, navigation connectivity, diagonal legality, shot obstruction, directional cover, contextual firing and the action revision. It is recorded and replays deterministically under a new simulation version.

Not done on purpose: autonomous AI door use, automatic opening during movement, locked doors, windows, historical replay compatibility, asset redesign, animation polish.

Design in one paragraph: the map is compiled once with every door in its authored state and once per door with that door flipped, by the same compiler and resolver. The cells that differ are the door's affected region (found automatically, including the cell beside the doorway that the swung leaf touches). Per state, the region's door-owned fields, the leaf's movement parts and its shot obstacles are kept. `MapDoors.apply` swaps them, rebuilds the LOS index and movement-part index, and `apply_with_graph` rebuilds the pathfinder graph through the existing `MapGraphBuilder.build` with a backup, a self-check and rollback. Dynamic geometry is therefore compared against independently compiled static maps instead of being a second model. The 06.08 static open/closed experiments are preserved as reference cases.

## 4. Policy as implemented (owner-approved)

- Explicit open/close; orthogonal adjacency plus physical reach; all factions.
- Rejected when: door already in that state, actor invalid or defeated, carrying unit, doorway occupied, another unit hit by the swept sector or either end pose of the leaf, any unit standing on a cell the door changes, not the active unit, stale revision, or reach/region failure (section 5).
- A rejection changes nothing: AP, RNG, occupancy, door state, revision and transaction counter are verified identical before and after.
- The operating unit is checked only against the leaf's end pose (it may open a door that swings toward it). Door actions do not cancel Aim, Shield or Guard. A 0-AP active unit may operate a door; free actions do not generate turns, restore resources or reset Guard eligibility.
- Opening provokes hostile armed Guard watchers only when the transition creates a newly legal shot against the opener (not legal before, legal after). Existing eligibility, supply, limits and ordering stay authoritative (fixed faction/id order). Closing never provokes. The door stays open after a lethal reaction.
- The open leaf is a real physical object for movement, LOS and cover. Windows stay static apertures.

## 5. Acceptance addendum: what was investigated and changed

### 5.1 Integration
See section 2.

### 5.2 Does the graph rebuild discard runtime navigation state? (one defect found and fixed)
Read from source: the graph is rebuilt only from map data (cells and `traversal_links`) through the same path the extraction planner already uses, so anything stored in the map survives (extraction transport footprints, cells closed by other systems, traversal links). `Pathfinder.live_grid` is explicitly preserved, the step limit is not reset by `clear()`, and occupancy is read live rather than stored in the graph. `graph_revision` advances, which is the intended cache invalidation. The only reservations in the codebase are AI squad reservations, which are not stored in the pathfinder.

Confirmed defect: `MapDoors.apply` wrote every geometric field of the region cells from the compile-time snapshot, so anything another system had changed on those cells after compilation (for example an extraction footprint beside a door) would have been silently reverted. Fix: write only the fields this door changes between its two states, and refuse the transition (`region_modified`) if another system has rewritten one of those door-owned fields. Tests prove: a non-door field of a region cell survives two transitions; a rewritten door-owned field refuses the action and a direct `apply` changes nothing; every graph edge away from the door's neighbourhood is identical after open and close; a cell closed by another system stays closed; transport footprints and `live_grid` are intact.

Limit: arbitrary traversal links cannot be injected on this map (links are physically validated and the range is flat), so link preservation rests on the shared rebuild path, not a door-specific test. I did not run the old code against the new test, so the defect is established by reading `apply` rather than by a red-then-green run.

### 5.3 Physical reach
The old 0.1 m sweep only proved the actor's own spot was clear. It now sweeps from the actor's standing point until the body touches the near face of the leaf (distance to the leaf minus the swept body radius, computed with the navigation sweep's own radius formula), over supported floor, so an obstruction or missing floor between actor and leaf refuses the action as `out_of_reach`. Negative control: a low crate flush against the leaf in the actor's cell. The old probe still reports the way clear past it; the new check refuses it through `MapDoors.check` and through the service query, and accepts again once the crate is removed. Honest scale: the body cannot physically get closer than about 0.18 m to the leaf face, so the added reach is small by geometry; the margin between the crate and the old probe is about 0.02 m.

### 5.4 End-to-end Guard replay (`tests/dynamic_door_guard_replay_test.gd`, 28 checks)
A real recorded sequence on seed 4: the player walks to the watch cell and sets Guard each round; the enemy walks to the door and opens it; four rounds; each opening creates a newly legal shot and a Guard reaction (25 damage each); the fourth is lethal; closing between rounds provoked nothing. The current-version recording is saved, validated and replayed in a fresh battle. Every action verifies; RNG state, door state and geometry signature, the watcher's HP/supply/Guard, the opener's HP and removal, battle result and action revision all equal the original. Control: flipping a recorded reaction outcome is detected. Seed note: seeds 8, 10 and 12 miss a shot and the watcher runs out of supply before a kill, so seed 4 is pinned (the test accepts a seed argument). AI controllers are removed in the recording battle so enemy actions are scripted; the replay battle is driven only by the recording.

### 5.5 Transition cost (`docs/quality/evidence/interaction-range/door_timing_probe.gd`, 30 rounds, 512-cell map, headless, median)

| Part | Time |
|---|---|
| Validation (`check`) | 0.05 ms |
| Apply (cells, parts, obstacles, LOS index) | 0.9 ms |
| Graph rebuild | 48.7 ms |
| Whole geometry transaction (`apply_with_graph`) | 49.5 ms |
| `submit_door` end to end, presentation suppressed | 57.2 ms |
| Animation (Kenney clip, presentation only) | 1.00 s open, 1.00 s close |

Authoritative work is about 5% of the animation and happens once per action before the animation starts. The graph rebuild dominates (about 0.1 ms per cell), so it grows with map size; a map several times larger would take a few hundred milliseconds, a visible hitch. Not optimized: no problem measured on the supported map, and no incremental-graph framework was wanted. Recorded as a risk. Headless timing excludes rendering.

## 7. Replay policy
Historical recordings are not supported. Current simulation and policy versions are bumped; an older recording is rejected by name with a clear message (tested). Door identities and states are part of the fingerprint only on maps that have doors, so other maps' fingerprints are unchanged. No migration code exists.

## 8. Decisions the owner accepted, and limitations that remain

Accepted: operator checked against end pose only; Aim/Shield/Guard preserved; carriers cannot operate doors; conservative rejection when a unit stands on any cell the door changes; the corrected leaf geometry.

Remaining limitations and risks:
- One-cell-thick tactical walls and the mesh-bake pipeline remain the accepted compromise; the Parcel Editor was not touched.
- Graph rebuild is full-map (about 50 ms here; scales with cells).
- The HUD button refreshes on action, phase and resource events, not on arbitrary occupancy changes.
- Guard reaction replay is end to end for one scenario (seed 4, one watcher, lethal); other reaction orders (multiple watchers) are covered at service level only.
- Extraction transport and pathfinder reservations are preserved by construction and by the extraction-footprint-style tests, but not exercised with a real extraction on a map that has a door.
- Evidence is automated (headless runs and one scripted interface walkthrough), not a human playtest. Headless success does not establish visual correctness.
- Pre-existing failures not caused by this work: `camera_environment_apertures_test` (fails identically on the baseline before the door work) and `tactical_archetype_lab_test` (verified identical on baseline 315c76c).
