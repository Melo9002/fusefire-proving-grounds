## Architecture (where to read)

| Concern | Source |
|---|---|
| Door state and per-state geometry | `systems/environment/door_data.gd` |
| Check / apply / rollback / graph rebuild | `systems/environment/map_doors.gd` |
| Registry on the map, `door_id` on cells | `systems/grid/map_data.gd`, `map_cell_data.gd` |
| Compile every state | `systems/generation2/authored_scene_map_compiler.gd` (`door_overrides`), `authored_battlefield_layout.gd` |
| Action lifecycle | `tactical_action_service.gd`: `query_door`, `submit_door`, `_fire_guard_reaction` |
| Results / replay record | `door_action_query_result.gd`, `door_action_result.gd` |
| Replay | `battle_replay_player.gd`, `battle_state_fingerprint.gd`, `replay_record_tools.gd`, `battle_replay_recording.gd` |
| Presentation and HUD | `door_presenter.gd`, `tactical_action_presenter.gd`, `action_hud_controller.gd` |

Design: the map is compiled once with every door in its authored state and once per door with that door flipped. Cells that differ between the two compilations are the affected region, found automatically (this includes the neighbour cell the swung leaf touches). `MapDoors.apply` swaps in the other state's cell geometry, movement parts and shot obstacles, rebuilds the LOS and movement-part indices, and rebuilds the pathfinder graph, with a backup and rollback if any step or the self-check fails. Dynamic geometry is therefore compared against independently compiled static maps rather than being a separate model.

Policy: open/close are free (0 AP), need the active unit, reject if the state already matches, the actor is invalid or carrying, the doorway is occupied, or the swinging leaf would intersect another unit. Rejections change nothing (AP, RNG, revision, occupancy, door state, transaction counter). Opening provokes Guard only for hostile armed watchers whose shot against the opener was illegal before and legal after; closing never does. Existing Guard eligibility, limits and ordering are unchanged (shared `_fire_guard_reaction`).

Replay: simulation version `prototype-1.5-doors-13`, policy `prototype-1.5-doors-policy-14`. Older recordings are rejected by name; no migration.

## Decisions for the owner

- The operating unit is checked only against the leaf's end pose, so it may open a door that swings toward it; every other unit is checked against the sweep and both poses.
- Door actions do not cancel Aim, Shield or Guard.
- Carrying units cannot operate doors.
- A 0-AP active unit may operate a door; a non-active unit may not.
- Any unit on a cell the door changes blocks the transition (conservative).

## Limitations and risks

- One-cell-thick tactical walls and the mesh-bake pipeline remain the accepted compromise; nothing here changes the Parcel Editor.
- Guard reactions on door opening are tested at service level and by resolved-result comparison, not end to end through a scripted replay.
- The graph rebuild could reset extraction transport or pathfinder reservations; not relevant on this map, untested elsewhere.
- The HUD refreshes on action, phase and resource events, not on arbitrary occupancy changes.
- Windows stay static; locked doors, keys, forced entry and breakable glass are out of scope.

## Follow-ups for 06.11 (AI)

AI caches are verified to observe committed door changes (no stale routes or firing evaluations), but the AI does not decide to open or close doors, does not plan through closed doors, and automatic opening during movement (the hybrid path) is deferred. Dynamic doors are **not** claimed complete for AI.

### 2. Does rebuilding the graph discard runtime state? (investigated, one real defect found and fixed)

The graph is rebuilt only from map data (`MapGraphBuilder.build`: cells, `traversal_links`), so anything living in the map survives: extraction transport footprints (`transport_footprints`, written into cell fields and rebuilt by the extraction planner the same way), cells closed by other systems, traversal links, and `Pathfinder.live_grid` (explicitly kept). The step limit is not reset by `clear()`. Occupancy is read live, not stored in the graph. `graph_revision` advances, which is the intended cache invalidation.

Confirmed defect: `MapDoors.apply` overwrote **every** geometric field of the door's region cells with the compile-time snapshot, so anything another system had changed on those cells after compilation (for example an extraction footprint placed beside a door) would be silently reverted. Fix: only the fields the door itself changes between its two states are written, and if another system has rewritten one of those door-owned fields the transition is refused (`region_modified`) instead of overwriting it. Rollback is unchanged. Tests (`dynamic_door_test`): an edit to a non-door field of a region cell survives two transitions; a rewritten door-owned field refuses the action and a direct `apply` changes nothing; every graph edge away from the door is identical after open and close; a cell closed by another system stays closed; footprints and `live_grid` are intact. Not testable on this map: arbitrary traversal links (they are physically validated and the range is flat), so link preservation rests on the shared rebuild path that extraction already uses.

### 3. Physical reach

The old 0.1 m sweep only proved the actor's own spot was clear. The check now sweeps from the actor's standing point until the body touches the near face of the leaf (distance to the leaf minus the swept body radius), over supported floor, so an obstruction or a missing floor between actor and leaf refuses the action as `out_of_reach`. Negative control: a low crate flush against the leaf in the actor's cell. The old probe still reports the way clear past it; the new check refuses, through `MapDoors.check` and the service query, and accepts again once the crate is removed. The body cannot physically get closer than about 0.18 m to the leaf face, so the added margin is small by geometry, not by laziness.

### 4. End-to-end Guard replay (`dynamic_door_guard_replay_test`, 28 checks)

A real recorded sequence (seed 4): the player walks to the watch cell and sets Guard each round, the enemy walks to the door and opens it, four rounds, each opening a newly legal shot with a Guard reaction (25 damage each), the fourth lethal; closing in between provoked nothing. The current-version recording is saved, validated and replayed in a fresh battle: every action verifies, and RNG state, door state and geometry signature, the watcher's HP, supply and Guard, the opener's HP and removal, battle result and action revision all equal the original. Negative control: flipping a recorded reaction outcome is detected. Other even seeds (8, 10, 12) miss a shot and run out of supply before a kill, so the seed is pinned.

### 5. Transition cost (`docs/quality/evidence/interaction-range/door_timing_probe.gd`, 30 rounds, 512-cell map, headless)

| Part | Median |
|---|---|
| Validation (`check`) | 0.05 ms |
| Apply (cells, parts, obstacles, LOS index) | 0.9 ms |
| Graph rebuild | 48.7 ms |
| Whole geometry transaction (`apply_with_graph`) | 49.5 ms |
| `submit_door` end to end, presentation suppressed | 57.2 ms |
| Animation (Kenney clip, presentation only) | 1.00 s open, 1.00 s close |

The authoritative work is about 5% of the animation and happens once per door action, before the animation starts. Dominated by the full graph rebuild (about 0.1 ms per cell), so it grows with map size; a map several times larger would take a few hundred milliseconds, a visible hitch. Not optimized: no measured problem on the supported map, and the owner asked for no incremental-graph framework. Recorded as a risk for large maps.
